Symbols, units, and reference values
====================================

units
-----
  SI throughout: m, s, Pa, m^3 (mL where stated), m^2 s^-1 for
  circulation. z points up and depth is d = -z. The ring is followed
  from its release depth d0 to d_e = 0.5 m, where the free surface
  lies more than twice the largest ring radius above the center.

reference values (illustrative, not calibrated)
-----------------------------------------------
  sea water density rho ............................... 1025.0 kg m^-3
  gravity g ........................................... 9.81 m s^-2
  surface tension gamma ............................... 0.073 N m^-1
  surface pressure p_atm .............................. 101325.0 Pa
  release depth d0 .................................... 10.0 m
  ring radius R0 ...................................... 0.05 m
  gas volume at release V0 ............................ 10 mL
  circulation Gamma ................................... 0.06 m^2 s^-1
  polytropic exponent ................................. 1 (isothermal)
  end depth d_e ....................................... 0.5 m

derived at release
------------------
  core radius a0 = (V0/(2 pi^2 R0))^(1/2) ............. 3.183 mm
  slenderness a0/R0 ................................... 0.0637
  ln(8R0/a0) - 1/2 = L0 ............................... 4.3336
  Boyle length ell_B = 2 V0 p0/(rho Gamma^2 L0) ....... 25.25 cm
  release pressure number rho g d0/p_atm .............. 0.9924
  swirl speed u_c = Gamma/(2 pi a0) ................... 3.000 m s^-1
  swirl Weber number .................................. 402.2
  core Froude number .................................. 288.2
  swirl head u_c^2/(2g) ............................... 0.459 m
  swirl pressure deficit / p0 ......................... 0.0228
  circulation Reynolds number Gamma/nu ................ 6e+04
  rise speed at release ............................... 0.414 m s^-1
  thermal time a0^2/kappa_gas ......................... 0.507 s
  pressure time p0/(rho g U0) ......................... 48.5 s
  ratio (isothermal if << 1) .......................... 0.0104
