reading
-------
  Absolute radii, times and drifts scale with the unmeasured gas
  volume, radius and circulation. The gain over a constant-volume ring
  depends mainly on the release pressure number, and the self-leveling
  law sin(theta) R^2 = const holds in every case because it follows
  from the impulse balance alone.
