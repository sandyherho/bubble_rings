Figure notes and tabulated values
=================================

purpose
-------
  Figures carry no in-panel numbers. Values that would otherwise be
  printed inside an axes are recorded here; every panel is also
  available as CSV under outputs/data.

figure 3, tilt
--------------
  lateral travel by 0.5 m (exact ideal gas),
  theta0 = 15, 30, 45, 60 deg:
    d0 = 5 m: 0.55, 1.10, 1.66, 2.22 m
    d0 = 10 m: 0.73, 1.45, 2.15, 2.83 m
    d0 = 20 m: 0.87, 1.73, 2.55, 3.32 m

  sin(theta) = const (the closure of Chang and Llewellyn Smith 2018)
  instead of R^2 sin(theta) = const.
